About

I'm a developer and creator. I enjoy turning ideas into things people can play, see, and use, and I'm always looking for new ways to improve my skills and learn something new.

I want to work with other developers and creators, share ideas, learn from them, and help bring creative visions to life.

Helping people matters a lot to me. I know what it's like to need guidance and not have anyone there, so I try to be the person I wish I'd had. If you're learning to code, working on a project, or finding your footing as a creator, I'm happy to help. I want to grow alongside others and be part of a community where people learn, create, and succeed together.
